## Calibration takeaways

- **Source Faithfulness consistently 4-5/5** when both videos have Gemini analysis. The Gemini schema fix removed a major false-negative on Faithfulness (it had been counting unanalyzed clips as "hallucinations").
- **Topic Boundary Respect is the bellwether dimension.** When it's 5/5 (shorts-mashup) the curriculum passes. When it's 2-3/5 it fails. Most of our work tonight targets this directly.
- **Step Flow stuck at 3/5 on every fixture** because of the WATCH-step fragmentation. See OPEN-FINDINGS — this is the headline open item.
- **The judge's verdicts are stable across runs.** Same fixture + same generation produces nearly identical scores. Variance is in the LLM generation itself, not the judge.
- **Parallel Gemini doesn't degrade quality.** Verified by comparing pre/post-parallelize runs on the same fixture shape. Speedup is free.

## Big open finding — read this first next session

**WATCH-step fragmentation** (see OPEN-FINDINGS.md for full detail).

User-visible learner UX shows 6 separate "WATCH" cards per lesson when the LLM produces 6 segment-aligned sub-clips. Intended design is **1 WATCH step per lesson** with internal chapter markers in a single chained player. Same root cause as every "no WATCH step" judge complaint we've seen.

This is a ~800-line UI refactor in `apps/web/app/learn/[programId]/timeline.tsx` (the clip-fallback rendering block) and likely also a `MuxVideoPlayer` change for chained playback. Properly scoped, it's a 1-2 session focused effort. Not safely doable at midnight.

## Other open findings

1. **timeBasedSlices snap window too tight** in some cases (60s). When the arithmetic midpoint falls >60s from the nearest segment boundary, no snap happens and the cut straddles. Widening the window risks bad snaps elsewhere. Look at adaptive window sizing.
2. **Chapter title rule didn't fully fire on L4** of upper-body-workout (Kickback omission). Even with the new "NEVER UNDERSELL" language. May need an explicit example in the prompt or post-validation that checks coverage of subtopics.
3. **Cross-video interleaving** (the v1→v2→v1→v2 pattern). The within-video rule we added doesn't address this. May want a "complete one source video before starting another" rule, but it would conflict with current workflow-sequencing rule in obvious cases.
4. **Lesson 6 of workout-like-a-champ** was a single clip spanning 3 Gemini topics (Abductions, Lunges, Squats) but titled only after the first.
